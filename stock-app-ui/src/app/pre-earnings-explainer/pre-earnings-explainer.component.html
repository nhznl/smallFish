Research it implements

The live schedule, selection, sizing, T+7, and Risk-On gates come from the frozen Pre-Earnings Momentum studies. Open those research tabs for methodology, verdicts, and caveats:

  • Study 3 — Post-Earnings Risk-On — daily decisions with next-session fills. The live tool uses this Risk-On method.
  • Study 4 — Friday-only weekly batch — the live calendar: Thursday close cutoff, Friday open execution, with the published holiday shift.

Kill switch

The kill switch immediately blocks new broker submissions. It does not sell, flatten, or otherwise change positions. Scan, reconcile, and read-only views keep working so you can inspect state. Release it from the same button when you want submissions allowed again.

Reset stats

Reset stats wipes the local execution ledger — cycles, plans, fills, valuations, and audit — and local evaluator artifacts. Broker positions are unchanged. Use it during development to reinitialize. It requires typing RESET.
