About this scan

Wheel Scan uses local OHLCV history to show realized volatility, a 1σ move approximation, and historical expiry-ITM/touch frequencies per symbol × horizon. These are descriptive analytics, not trade selection or probabilities. The separate Option Quotes tab shows the immutable archive created by Collect Option Quotes. See the Wheel Explainer for definitions, boundaries, and sample-count guidance.
